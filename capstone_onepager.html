The problem
4–8h to abstract a single commercial lease manually
13 days total due diligence cycle per acquisition
€192k annual abstraction cost at 12 deals/year

Before each acquisition closes, the legal team manually reads and abstracts 10–30 commercial leases — extracting key dates, rent schedules, break options, and unusual clauses. Two past deals had clause errors with material financial consequences. Chleo's concern: "How do I know the AI isn't making things up?" That fear is shared by 44% of real estate investment committees. It is the right question, and the solution is built around it.

The solution
An AI-assisted lease review assistant that extracts structured data from lease PDFs and flags non-standard clauses — with every field traced to its source passage. The lawyer reviews and approves the output. The AI does the reading; the lawyer does the thinking.

Total: ~6 months from kick-off to full deployment. Pilot success criteria are defined before commitment — accuracy ≥ 90%, review time ≤ 45 min/lease, zero unchecked outputs entering a financial model.

Key risks & mitigations
AI hallucination
Every field cites source text. Human review mandatory before any output is used.
Lawyer adoption
Co-design output format with legal team. Pilot on a completed deal with known ground truth.
Document variability
Start pilot on digitally-authored leases. Extend to scanned documents in Phase 2.
EU AI Act / GDPR
Limited risk classification. Drafting aid, not decision system. Legitimate interest basis for GDPR.
